How this board is fed

An automated scraper runs automatically about every 5-10 minutes without anyone being online, and Refresh now starts an immediate scan. It reads regulator and risk-service pages (EASA CZIBs, Safe Airspace), news feeds (Al Jazeera, BBC, The National, Arab News, Middle East Eye, Times of Israel), aviation media (Aviation Herald, Simple Flying, Wego) and a set of Google News searches that aggregate Reuters, AP, AFP, Gulf News, Khaleej Times and others. Only items with a direct aviation impact are kept (airspace and FIR status, NOTAMs and CZIBs, airport attacks or closures, airline suspensions and diversions, threats to aircraft, GNSS interference). Items about the same incident are grouped into one event. Before an alert is shown it must be corroborated by at least two independent outlets or one official/risk source; single-source items are held back as "Pending verification" and cross-checked against other outlets. Reports that look like old news (past-year headlines, "recap/what we know" wording, headlines dated days earlier) are rejected, and later re-reports of an older incident are attached to it without raising a new alert.

Confidence follows the best source: CONFIRMED (regulator or authority), RELIABLE (risk service, or 3+ independent reports), REPORTED (news agency), UNVERIFIED (individual reporter only).

Open-source awareness only. Check official NOTAMs, AIP SUPs and CZIBs before any operational decision.
